Growatt Error 102 Troubleshooting

How to Fix Growatt Error 102 on Your Inverter

WHA By WHA SOL Technical Team • Updated September 19, 2026 • 8 min read

Growatt Error 102 is one of the more serious fault codes you can encounter on a Growatt inverter. Unlike a temporary glitch that clears itself after a reboot, this error signals an internal mismatch that the inverter cannot simply power-cycle its way out of. If you're seeing this code in Pakistan — where Growatt remains one of the most widely installed inverter brands — understanding what it means and what to do about it can save you hours of confusion and unnecessary service calls.

Important Safety Warning

Before attempting any action on your inverter, ensure you understand the correct shutdown sequence (DC isolator first, then AC). An incorrect power-down procedure can trigger additional faults or cause physical damage. If in doubt, contact a qualified solar technician.

Fault Classification: Consistent Fault

Error 102 on a Growatt inverter is classified as a Consistent Fault. This means there is a persistent mismatch between the data reported by the inverter's master processor and its slave processor. These two processors constantly cross-check each other's readings to ensure the system operates safely. When their data doesn't line up, the system flags a Consistent Fault and halts operation entirely — you'll see the red fault indicator light up, and the unit will stop producing power.

What Causes It

Several underlying issues can trigger the master-to-slave processor mismatch that produces Error 102:

  • Internal communication failure — the data bus between the two processors is interrupted or corrupted.
  • Sensor drift — one processor reads a value (voltage, current, temperature) that deviates beyond the accepted threshold from the other.
  • Firmware inconsistency — after a failed update or partial flash, the two processors may run different firmware versions.
  • Power supply instability — irregular DC input from the panels or a failing battery can cause erratic readings on one side.
  • Component degradation — aging capacitors or ICs on one processor board can introduce timing or signal errors.

Step 1: Try a Power Cycle

Before assuming the worst, attempt a full shutdown and restart of the inverter. In some cases this clears a temporary processor sync issue and restores normal function. Make sure you follow the correct shutdown sequence (DC isolator first, then AC, in the proper order) rather than just cutting power — an incorrect sequence can trigger additional faults or damage the unit.

For the complete step-by-step shutdown and restart procedure, see here.

Step 2: If the Fault Persists

A repeat Error 102 after a reboot usually points to an internal hardware fault rather than a temporary glitch. At this stage, calling in your installer or a certified technician is the next move rather than continuing to power-cycle it yourself.

Your technician will likely:

  • Check the firmware versions on both processors and flash if needed.
  • Inspect the communication bus and connectors between the processor boards.
  • Test the sensor inputs (voltage, current, temperature) for drift or failure.
  • Examine the DC power supply for instability that could cause inconsistent readings.
  • Determine whether one processor board needs replacement.

Check Your Warranty Status First

Still within warranty period

Growatt inverters sold through authorized distributors in Pakistan typically carry a manufacturer warranty (commonly 5 years, sometimes longer depending on model and dealer). Keep your original purchase invoice and installation details handy — you'll need these along with the inverter's model/serial number and the fault code when filing a claim. Contact your dealer or Growatt's authorized service center to initiate the warranty process. Attempting to open the inverter yourself will void the warranty.

Out of warranty

If the warranty period has lapsed, repair costs fall on you. Finding a technician who works specifically on Growatt's internal boards can be difficult outside major cities, so check with your original installer or local solar suppliers in Lahore, Karachi, or Islamabad who often handle authorized service and replacement parts. Based on the nature of Error 102 — an internal processor mismatch — repair costs can range from moderate (if it's a connector or sensor issue) to significant (if a processor board needs replacing). Get a detailed quote before approving any work.

Bottom Line

Growatt remains a popular choice in Pakistan's solar market for its price-to-performance balance. If your unit is out of warranty and throwing a persistent internal fault like Error 102, replacing it with a new inverter — ideally from an authorized local dealer with proper after-sales support — is usually more practical than chasing a hard-to-source repair.

Before making a final decision, have a qualified technician confirm the diagnosis in writing. A second opinion ensures you aren't replacing a unit that could have been fixed for a fraction of the cost.

Pro Tip

Always write down the exact error code and any additional messages displayed on your inverter's screen before calling a technician. The more information you can provide, the faster the diagnosis and the lower the diagnostic charge.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Error 102 clear itself on its own?

In rare cases, a temporary communication glitch may clear on its own after a full, properly sequenced shutdown and restart. However, if the fault returns within a few days, it is almost certainly a hardware issue that needs professional attention.

Will my solar system produce power while Error 102 is active?

No. When Error 102 is flagged, the inverter stops producing power entirely as a safety precaution. The panels continue generating DC electricity, but it cannot be converted to AC until the fault is cleared.

Is Error 102 covered under Growatt warranty in Pakistan?

Yes, if the inverter is within the warranty period and the fault is determined to be a manufacturing or design defect, it should be covered. Physical damage or unauthorized modifications typically void the claim. Check with your authorized dealer for specific terms.

How long does a repair take?

If parts are available locally, a processor board replacement or communication fix can usually be completed within a few hours. If parts need to be ordered from overseas, expect 1–2 weeks depending on logistics.

Should I switch brands when replacing?

Not necessarily. If you're happy with Growatt's performance and support in your area, staying with the same brand for a new unit can simplify future maintenance. However, it's worth comparing quotes and warranties from other brands before committing.
