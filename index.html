WHA SOL Proactive Strategies

A Maintenance Plan That Pays for Itself

Routine strategy keeps production high and downtime near zero. Follow a simple, seasonal approach that any keeper can run.

  1. 01

    Inspect Monthly

    Check output, visible damage and error history in your monitoring app.

  2. 02

    Clean Quarterly

    Wash panels in the early morning to remove dust, pollen and bird residue.

  3. 03

    Terminate Annually

    Torque-check all connections and test earthing with a certified electrician.

  4. 04

    Record & React

    Log every reading so drops in performance are caught before they cost you.
